About Us

A small shop with a big love for coffee

Our Story

Corner Cup Coffee started as a small family-run shop with one goal: serve good coffee at a fair price. Today we are a neighborhood spot where students study, workers grab breakfast on the way in, and friends meet up on the weekend.

We use locally roasted beans and bake our pastries every morning.
