Applications

JavaScript projects built during the course

App 1

Image Slider

A photo slideshow that shows our menu specials. It moves to the next photo every 4 seconds, and you can use the arrows, click a thumbnail, or pause it.

App 2

Coffee Order Calculator

Pick an item from our menu and enter how many you want. The calculator shows the subtotal, NYC sales tax and the total. It also checks that an item is chosen and the quantity is at least 1.

App 3

Contact Form Validation

The contact form checks that the name, email and message are filled in and that the email looks valid before showing a thank you message.

Try it on the Contact page
