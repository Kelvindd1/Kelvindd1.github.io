Calculate Your Monthly Payment

How It Works

Loan amount = home price - down payment

Monthly rate (r) = annual rate / 100 / 12

Number of payments (n) = years x 12

M = P x r(1 + r)n / ((1 + r)n - 1)

If the rate is 0%, the payment is just P / n.
